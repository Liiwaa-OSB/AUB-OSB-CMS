About Career Management Services

CMS supports students and alumni in their career development while connecting employers with emerging talent.

In an increasingly competitive career environment, CMS provides the guidance, resources, and opportunities to help you build your skills, navigate career challenges, and take confident steps toward your professional goals.

Contact CMS Meet the Team

Our mandate

At CMS, we:

i

Prepare students for a competitive and changing job market.

ii

Develop career skills, confidence, and workplace readiness.

iii

Connect students and alumni with jobs, internships, and career opportunities.

iv

Support alumni through career transitions and professional growth.

v

Partner with employers to connect them with talented students and graduates.
