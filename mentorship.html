OSB Graduate Mentorship Program

The OSB Graduate Mentorship Program pairs current students with alumni through a carefully curated, one on one matching process based on shared industry interests, career goals, and professional backgrounds.

Apply as a Mentor Apply as a Mentee

Program overview

Real career questions, answered together

Throughout the program, mentors and mentees meet regularly to navigate real career questions, from making career decisions to understanding the unwritten rules of the industries they aspire to enter.

Mentors

For Alumni

As an OSB alumnus, your professional experience is a valuable resource for the next generation of business leaders. By joining the OSB Graduate Mentorship Program as a mentor, you will guide ambitious students, share the insights that shaped your own career, and contribute to developing business talent across the region and beyond, all while expanding your professional network and strengthening your leadership capabilities.

Mentees

For Students

The OSB Graduate Mentorship Program connects you with an alumnus positioned to support your professional development. Through structured, one on one mentorship, you will gain career clarity, industry insight, and constructive feedback, along with access to a professional network that extends well beyond graduation.
