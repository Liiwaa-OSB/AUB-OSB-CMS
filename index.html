Get to Know CMS

Career Management Services (CMS), part of the Suliman S. Olayan School of Business, supports students and alumni in navigating their career journeys and preparing for professional success.

  • Personalized career guidance
  • CV and interview preparation
  • Internship and employment opportunities
  • Professional development workshops
  • Networking events

CMS equips students and alumni with the tools, skills, and connections to advance their careers. CMS also works closely with employers and industry partners to bridge the gap between academic learning and the professional world.

Make your next career move with confidence.

In an increasingly competitive career environment, CMS provides the guidance, resources and opportunities to help you build your skills, navigate career challenges and take confident steps toward your professional goals.

We support students and alumni in their career development while connecting employers with emerging talent.

  1. Prepare

    Prepare students for a competitive and changing job market.

  2. Develop

    Develop career skills, confidence and workplace readiness.

  3. Connect

    Connect students and alumni with jobs, internships and career opportunities.

  4. Support

    Support alumni through career transitions and professional growth.

  5. Partner

    Partner with employers to reach talented students and graduates.
