Questions

Answers to the questions students ask us most often.

How do I find an internship?

Start by exploring internship opportunities through the university's career portal, career fairs, employer events, and networking opportunities. You can also speak with a career advisor who can help you identify suitable opportunities and guide you through the application process.

When can I apply for an internship?

You can start looking for internships as early as your first or second year of study. Many employers recruit several months before the internship begins, so it's a good idea to start your search early and check application deadlines regularly.

What if I don't know what career I want?

That's completely normal. Career advisors can help you explore your interests, skills, values, and career options. Through career assessments, one-on-one guidance, workshops, and conversations with professionals and alumni, you can gain a better understanding of the paths that may be right for you.

How can I make my CV stand out?

Tailor your CV to each position by highlighting relevant skills and experiences. Use the resources on our website for guidance or book an appointment with a career advisor for personalized support.

How do I get ready for my first interview?

Research the organization and role, practice common interview questions, and book a mock interview with a career advisor to build confidence.

Where can I find job opportunities?

Find job opportunities through the university's career portal, career fairs, employer events, company websites, and alumni network. You can also join our mailing list to receive the latest opportunities and career updates.

How can I connect with employers and alumni?

Attend career fairs, employer events, and networking opportunities, and connect with alumni through the university's network. Career Services can help you build meaningful connections.

Can I get career support after graduation?

Yes! Alumni can continue to access career support and benefit from the OSB Graduate Mentorship Program. Contact us to learn more about the resources available to you.

How can I build my professional network?

Start building your network by attending events, connecting with alumni and professionals, and maintaining relationships with your industry connections.

How can I connect with alumni working in my field of interest?

Explore our Alumni section to discover graduates working in industries and roles that interest you. You can also connect with alumni through the OSB Graduate Mentorship Program to gain career insights and advice.

How can I discover the career path that is right for me?

Start by exploring your interests, strengths, values, and goals. Try different experiences through internships, part-time jobs, volunteering, student activities, and career events. Speaking with career advisors, professors, and alumni can also help you understand different career paths and make informed decisions.
